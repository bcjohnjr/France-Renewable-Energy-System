# Journal selection (V20)
**Chosen:** Research & Design of Nuclear Engineering (RDNE) — EDP Sciences, Les Ulis, France; eISSN 3095-7788; launched 2025; CC BY open access; submission via rdne.nestor-edp.org.
- **Fees:** the launch announcement states no publication charges for accepted articles in the initial post-launch period, and the charges page lists no page charges. The generic author instructions still mention APCs after acceptance, so **get written confirmation that no APC applies if acceptance falls in 2027.**
- **Scope fit:** the scope includes “comprehensive utilization of nuclear energy” and “nuclear policy, economic and human resource development”. Cogeneration and desalination are not named, so the cover letter frames the paper under those two headings.
- **Things you should know:** RDNE is published on behalf of China Nuclear Power Engineering, its Editor-in-Chief is from CNPE, and EDP Sciences is owned by Science Press (China). It is French-published but not French-edited. It is new, with no impact factor yet.
- **Format applied:** Original Article; abstract ≤ 300 words without abbreviations or citations (EN 268); 6 keywords; IMRaD and mandatory declarations; numbered references with DOIs; double spacing, continuous line numbers; tables one per page with captions above; AI use disclosed in the manuscript and cover letter; six-part cover letter (no suggested reviewers required).

**Why not EPJ N:** per the referee, its APC is €800 unless waived, which conflicts with your no-fee requirement.
**Alternatives:** STET (no fee, best scope) is closed to new submissions; Comptes Rendus Mécanique (Académie des sciences, diamond OA, accepts French) is the strictly French option but a weaker fit unless the paper is cut down to the thermal analysis.
